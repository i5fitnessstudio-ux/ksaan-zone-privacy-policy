We use personal information only for legitimate purposes connected with the KSAAN Zone service, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users and maintaining account security
  • Managing student profiles
  • Managing attendance records
  • Managing fee-related records and administrative information
  • Processing leave requests
  • Sending service-related notifications and updates
  • Providing customer and technical support
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the application
  • Troubleshooting errors and improving application performance
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, or administrative requirements

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Personal information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate KSAAN Zone, provide a requested service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of users and the application. Where used, such recipients may include:

  • Cloud hosting, server, database, or infrastructure service providers
  • Notification, email, or messaging service providers used to deliver application communications
  • Technical support or service providers that assist with operating or maintaining the application
  • Authorized educational or administrative personnel who require access for legitimate operational purposes
  • Government authorities, regulators, law-enforcement agencies, or courts when disclosure is required by applicable law

Service providers are expected to process information only for the purposes for which it is provided and to apply appropriate safeguards.

6. Data Security and Handling

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These safeguards may include authenticated access to user accounts, restricted access to administrative systems and databases, server and database security controls, and secure network transmission where supported by the production environment.

Access to personal information is limited to authorized persons and systems that require the information for legitimate operational purposes. We do not intentionally expose passwords, authentication credentials, or other sensitive account information to unauthorized users.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so while we take reasonable precautions, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide KSAAN Zone services, maintain user accounts and administrative records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or misuse, meet security requirements, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it will be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with our operational and legal requirements. Backup copies may remain for a limited period until they are overwritten or deleted as part of normal backup processes.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their KSAAN Zone account and associated personal information through the account or settings area of the application where that option is available, or by contacting us at:

Email: support@ksaanzone.com

We may verify the identity of the person making the request before processing deletion. After a valid deletion request is processed, personal information associated with the account will be removed from active systems unless certain information must be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, security, or regulatory purposes. Any information retained for such purposes will be kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

9. Notifications

KSAAN Zone may send service-related notifications, reminders, account updates, attendance information, fee-related information, or other operational communications. Users may manage notification preferences through the application or device settings where such controls are available.

10. Children's Privacy

KSAAN Zone is intended for use in connection with educational or institutional services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization from a parent, guardian, educational institution, or other authorized party where such authorization is required.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to review and delete the information where required.

11. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, users may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with their account. Users may also contact us with questions about how their personal information is handled.
