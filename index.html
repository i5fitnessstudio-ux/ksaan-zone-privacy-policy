Delete your ParkShare account

Instructions for vehicle users and parking space owners to permanently delete their account and associated personal information.

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion directly from the ParkShare mobile app:

  1. Open ParkShare and Log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Home screen.
  3. If necessary, refresh the app. If you are asked to sign in again, Log in again.
  4. Open your account or profile area and select Delete Account.
  5. Read the deletion warning and confirm that you want to continue.
  6. Enter your account password to verify your identity.
  7. Tap Delete Account Permanently.
  8. After successful deletion, ParkShare displays a confirmation and returns you to the account selection screen.
Important: This action is permanent. Your ParkShare account will be deactivated and your personal account information anonymized. You will no longer be able to sign in to the deleted account.

Data deleted or anonymized

When an account deletion succeeds, ParkShare processes the following information:

  • Account details: your name and email are replaced with non-identifying placeholders; phone number and saved UPI ID are cleared; your previous password is invalidated.
  • Vehicle users: saved vehicle records are deleted, and their uploaded vehicle photos are scheduled for removal.
  • Parking owners: listings without booking history are deleted. Listings with booking history are disabled and their descriptive and location fields are anonymized.
  • Messages and security data: conversations, refresh tokens and password-reset codes associated with your account are deleted.
  • Upcoming activity: eligible pending, confirmed and active bookings linked to your account are cancelled.

Data retained and why

Some non-active records are retained to maintain the integrity of booking and payment history and to meet any applicable financial, tax, legal or dispute-handling obligations:

  • Booking history: historical booking records and booking-status history may be retained, including records of cancellations.
  • Payment records: necessary payment and transaction records may be retained. Parking owners' saved UPI IDs are cleared from the specified financial record fields.
  • Technical account record: an inactive, anonymized account record remains to preserve database relationships.
  • Listing media: some photos of historical parking listings may remain available with preserved booking history.

Retention period: Retained records are kept for as long as required by applicable obligations or legitimate recordkeeping needs. ParkShare has not specified a fixed retention period for these records on this page.

Need help?

If you have trouble completing account deletion, confirm you are signed in to the intended account and try the in-app steps again. Never share your password or OTP with anyone.

Visiting this information page does not delete an account. Account deletion is completed inside the ParkShare app.
